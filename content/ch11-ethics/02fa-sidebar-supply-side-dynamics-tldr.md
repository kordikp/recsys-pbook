A music app pushes new songs that listeners don't skip in the first 30 seconds. Within months, artists drop their intros and many new songs sound alike.

That is the **supply-side loop**: creators and sellers study what the ranking rewards and make more of it, so the objective shapes tomorrow's catalog. Reward raw engagement and you get look-alike and bait content, plus floods of cheap AI-generated uploads. In April 2026, Deezer said about 44% of its daily uploads were fully AI-generated, and it keeps detected ones out of algorithmic recommendations.

Healthy platforms track **supply metrics** (exposure for newcomers, creator retention, how concentrated plays are) and use levers: a guaranteed slice of slots for new creators, diversity re-ranking, and rewarding satisfaction and originality over clicks.
