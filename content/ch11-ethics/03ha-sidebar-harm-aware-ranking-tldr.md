A teenager looking for workout videos is fed, swipe by swipe, clips about skipping meals. Each clip is allowed; the **sequence** is the harm.

Platforms answer with three levers. **Remove** what breaks the rules. **Reduce** **borderline content**, meaning allowed but close to the line: it stays up, but the feed stops pushing it, by ranking it lower or showing it only to followers. **Inform** with labels and context.

Reduce matters because people tend to engage more with content the closer it gets to the line, so a feed tuned for clicks drifts toward it.

Success is **prevalence**: the share of views that landed on harmful content. Track the cost too: legitimate posts wrongly demoted.
