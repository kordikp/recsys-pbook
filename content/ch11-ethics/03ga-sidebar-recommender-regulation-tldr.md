Three questions decide whether the EU **Digital Services Act (DSA)** regulates your recommender.

1. **Do users publish to others through you?** Marketplaces and social networks do. A shop recommending only its own products usually doesn't. No → no DSA recommender duties.
2. **Fewer than 50 staff and at most €10 million turnover?** Then you are exempt.
3. **Otherwise:** your terms must explain, in plain language, what your recommendations are mainly based on and why, plus any options users have to change them.

Only **very large platforms** (45+ million monthly EU users, designated by the Commission) must also offer a feed not built from personal profiles.

Whatever the answers, **GDPR** applies: personalizing is profiling, so it needs a lawful basis. Consumer law applies too. Take your answers to counsel.
