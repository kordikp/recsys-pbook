On any streaming service, the catalog looks like this:

| Slice | Share of catalog | Share of plays |
|---|---|---|
| top 100 tracks | ~0.001 % | ~10 % |
| top 1 % | 1 % | ~60 % |
| everything else — the tail | 99 % | the rest, thinly spread |

Charts and radio can only ever serve the head — there's one Top 40 for everyone. A recommender is the only machine that can walk **each listener** into a different part of the tail: the Slovak jazz trio for you, the 1970s field recordings for someone else.

That's the business case in one line: the head everyone already knows; **the tail is inventory that only personalization can sell.** And it's the cultural case too — without recommenders, 99 % of music effectively doesn't exist.
