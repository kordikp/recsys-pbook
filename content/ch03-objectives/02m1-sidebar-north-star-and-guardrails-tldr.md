A recommender team has to agree on what "better" means before it changes anything.

The **north-star metric** is the one number you try to push up. Pick it because it tracks long-term value, such as customers who keep coming back or money left after returns, and not because it is easy to move. Clicks are easy to move. A misleading headline moves them too.

**Guardrail metrics** are numbers that must not get worse while you chase the north star: returns, complaints, the variety of what people see, page speed.

You need both because any single number can be raised in harmful ways. That gives the launch rule: **a new version ships only if the north star improves and every guardrail holds.**
