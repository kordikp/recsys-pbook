A news app can personalize at five levels. **Generic**: "most read today", the same for everyone. **Segment**: "popular in your district", the same for a group. **Contextual**: "read next" under the article you're reading, which works even for anonymous visitors. **Individual**: a "For you" tab built from your reading history. **Steerable**: you can ask for "more politics, less sport".

Each level needs more data than the one below. Pick one **per surface** with three questions: What do we know about this reader right now? Should different people see different things here at all (a flood warning shouldn't)? Does the higher level beat the lower one in an A/B test, where half the readers get each version? If not, stay on the lower rung.
