![The Record Shop Rearranges the Mood — a three-panel comic. Shelved by genre, a fan of a quiet jazz ballad is offered a 14-minute drum solo. The clerk notices the same people play the ballad and an ambient album, so he re-shelves the shop as a map learned from listening. The ballad's spot (its embedding) now sits next to ambient and whale songs, far from the drum solo.](images/comic-embeddings.svg)

*Embeddings are learned vectors: each item's spot on a map built from behavior. Nearby items behave alike, so finding recommendations is a fast nearest-neighbor (ANN) search.*
