The same algorithms behave differently from one industry to the next. Which domain is closest to your work or curiosity?

**A) "Video, music and podcasts"**
Content discovery for a streaming platform: what to watch or play next, and how long people stay with it.

**B) "E-commerce and deals"**
Product discovery, conversion, basket size, and offers that expire.

**C) "News and media"**
Information quality, editorial independence, and the societal side of algorithmic curation.

**D) "Marketplaces, real estate, travel, jobs and education"**
Unique items, two-sided matching, geography, and decisions people make rarely but care about a lot.

Each domain teaches something the others can use. Video's content hierarchy helps any domain with structured items, e-commerce's conversion funnel applies wherever there is a purchase, and news's diversity requirements matter everywhere fairness is a concern. The knowledge is portable; the constraints are what make each domain interesting.
