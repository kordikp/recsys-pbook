You scroll. You stop for 2.4 seconds on a climbing video. You scroll on. **That pause was a vote.**

| You did | Feed heard |
|---|---|
| paused mid-scroll | "look closer at this topic" |
| replayed 3 s of a clip | strong interest |
| shared to a friend | strongest signal there is |
| scrolled past fast | mild no |
| "not interested" tap | hard no — rare and precious |

Three kinds of clues combine: what the post **is** (item), who you **are** (person), what you **did** (action) — and on a feed, *action* dominates, because you produce hundreds per session without touching a button.

The upshot: you're always training it. The only question is whether on purpose.
