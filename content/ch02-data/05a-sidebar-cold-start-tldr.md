A **new item has zero interactions**, so "people who liked this also liked that" has nothing to work with.

The fix is a relay of two bridges. First, **content**: the item's title, tags and description say what it *is*, so the system can place it next to similar known items and show it to their fans. Second, **exploration**: the item gets a small budget of fair impressions to collect its first real clicks.

The moment genuine interactions arrive, behavioral signal takes over — cold start over.
