Job boards live in *permanent* cold start — postings expire in weeks, so the newest items are always the most important ones. Watch the loop: a five-minute-old **Data Engineer** posting → its skill requirements (*SQL, Python, ETL*) match two of three candidate profiles → exactly those two see it first → the first application ✓ teaches the ranker who else fits.
