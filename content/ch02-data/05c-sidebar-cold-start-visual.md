Read the picture left to right:

**1 — Blind.** The new item arrives with zero interactions. "People who liked this…" has no *this* to work with.

**2 — Bridged.** Its title, tags and description place it next to items it resembles — similar by what it **is**, not by who clicked it.

**3 — Learning.** A small exploration budget shows it to the likeliest fans; their first clicks are the signal that wakes collaborative filtering up.
