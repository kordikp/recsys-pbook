**How Recommendations Work** is a free, living book about recommender systems — the software that chooses what you see in shops, streaming apps and feeds. It is written by **Pavel Kordík** (co-founder of [Recombee](https://www.recombee.com) and associate professor at the Czech Technical University in Prague), **Eva Nečasová** ([AI dětem](https://aidetem.cz)) and the **Recombee team**, with AI models as collaborators.

**A disclosure:** Recombee sells a recommendation engine, and this book runs on it. Some chapters draw examples from Recombee's product and from research by Recombee Labs and FIT CTU. Read those as one vendor's perspective, not the only way to do it.

### How it is made

- **People own the facts.** For every concept, the authors write a *contract*: the question a reader must be able to answer afterwards, the points every telling must cover, and the claims no telling may make.
- **AI does the bookkeeping and helps write.** AI models from Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI (GPT) tag every telling with its *facets* — depth, length, example world, form, language — keep those tags in step when the text changes, and helped draft many tellings under the contracts. The authors edit the drafts and decide what is accepted.
- **Figures are AI-drawn and reviewed twice.** In version 2, a redrawn figure replaces the old one only after it wins a blind side-by-side comparison with it and the editors approve it.
- **Reader-made tellings are labelled.** In open mode (switch it on in Profile) you also see tellings generated on request or shared by other readers, marked *not yet editor-verified* until an editor adopts them.

### How the book adapts to you

This book is a recommender, and you are one of its users. Every telling is an item described by its facets. Opening, reading, liking and saving tellings are interactions, and Recombee uses them to fill your home shelves and your reading feed. Reading a telling, choosing another one or asking for a change also updates a profile of the facets you tend to prefer. You can see that profile in **Profile** and overrule it under **Format preferences**: explicit picks beat the learned model. At the top of a section, 🎛️ lists the other tellings of the same idea; at its end you can ask for a simpler, deeper or more visual one. The book logs reading events under a random reader id (or your account, if you sign in) to improve the book and for research on personalized books.

### How to cite it

> Kordík, P., Nečasová, E., & the Recombee team (2026). *How Recommendations Work: a living book about recommender systems* (version 2). https://recsys-pbook.vercel.app

The book keeps changing, so add the date you read it. To point at one idea, link its concept: `https://recsys-pbook.vercel.app/#c/item-cold-start`. On GitHub, *Cite this repository* gives the same reference in BibTeX.

### Work with us

If you want to write or translate tellings, teach with the book, run your own personalized book on this engine, or study it, use **🤝 Want to collaborate?** on the welcome screen or in ⚙️ Settings → About & contact; your message reaches only the authors. To report a mistake, use ⚑ under any section. Code and content live on [GitHub](https://github.com/kordikp/recsys-pbook), where issues and pull requests are welcome.
