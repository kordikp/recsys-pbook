An **AI agent** is an assistant that takes steps for you: it searches, compares and can even buy. It meets recommenders in two roles. As the **caller**, a shop's own assistant uses the shop's recommender as a tool: find matching products, rank them, explain the picks in words. As the **customer**, someone else's assistant shops at your store on a person's behalf.

Either way, a machine reads the list, and four things change. **Signals:** the agent's clicks are not the person's taste, so learn from what the person confirms, keeps or returns. **Ranking:** agents filter on facts like size, price, stock and delivery date, not photos. **Explanations:** give reasons the agent can check and repeat. **Trust:** product text can hide instructions meant to steer the agent, and paying needs the person's OK.

Open standards for agent tools already exist. Agents that shop for you are still new.
