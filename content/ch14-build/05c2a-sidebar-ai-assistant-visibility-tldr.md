Ask an assistant for "waterproof trail shoes for wide feet" and it works in two steps. First it **searches**: web pages, reviews and **product feeds** (the lists of products, prices and stock that shops send in). Then a language model **writes** an answer from what it found.

So your shoe must be **findable**: your pages open to the assistant's search crawler, your product data accurate. And it must be **readable**: the page should say in plain words "wide fit, waterproof, €139", because the model can only repeat claims it finds.

Answers change with every wording and every run, so there is no fixed "rank #1". Instead, ask 40 real customer questions several times a month and count **how often** you are mentioned, and whether the details are right. Hidden instructions aimed at the AI are spam, not a shortcut.
